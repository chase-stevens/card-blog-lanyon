Many different card games have many different types of cards. Creatures, minions, spells, instants, sorceries, monsters, heroes, traps, locations, enchantments, planeswalkers, artifacts. Keeping track of all of them is enough to make your head spin.

Don't worry, though. If you cut through the noise, you'll quickly find that a card is either a permanent, or an action.

## Permanent
A permanent is something that sticks around on the field when you play it. Take our friend Swab Goblin here.

![Swab Goblin](/assets/images/2026-03-09-permanents-vs-spells/fdn-548-swab-goblin.png){:.my-class}

When Swab Goblin is in your hand, you can pay two mana and play it. When you do so, it goes onto the battlefield and stays there until someone wins the game, or it meets its untimely end.

## Action
An action, on the other hand, is an effect that changes something about the game, and is immediately discarded.

Let's say our opponent has had enough of our Swab Goblin's shenanigans and decides to play Shock to remove it.
![Shock](/assets/images/2026-03-09-permanents-vs-spells/j25-598-shock.png){:.my-class}

Shock is an Instant card, which is a type of Action in Magic the Gathering. It would deal two damage to our Swab Goblin and then be discarded.

Our Swab Goblin has two toughness. When the effect of Shock happens, Swab Goblin will receive two damage, and then meet its untimely end.

## Stapling Actions Onto Permaments
Sometimes, a card can be a permanent that also does an action. Let's take a look at Goblin Commando.

![Goblin Commando](/assets/images/2026-03-09-permanents-vs-spells/jmp-325-goblin-commando.png){:.my-class}

Goblin Commando, like Swab Goblin, is a 2/2 creature, which makes it a permanent. When Goblin Commando enters, it deals two damage to target creature, like Shock. 

How can this be? If I want to get the most value out of my cards, why would I play two cards when I could play one instead? Or if I want the cheapest cards in my deck, why would I have a five mana card when I can play a two mana card and a one mana card instead?

This highlights the tradeoffs between speed and value. Goblin Commando, for most intents and purposes, is a Swab Goblin that has a Shock stapled onto it. Those two cards are combined into one card, and you get more value out of one card.

## Sneaking Permanents Into Actions
On the other hand, a card can be an action that makes permanents. Here's Krenko's Command. 

![Krenko's Command](/assets/images/2026-03-09-permanents-vs-spells/m13-139-krenko-s-command.png){:.my-class}

Here, we have an action that has an effect to make permanents. When you play Krenko's Command, you do the effect and discard the card, but the end result is that you have permanents on your side of the fiend.
