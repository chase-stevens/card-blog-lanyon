Welcome to the Card Collection! This here is a blog examining one of the most foundational and formative game components - the card.

Cards are simple. They have a side with information and a side that's hidden. They can be randomized quickly and mixed and matched.  They can be bent, played, flipped, any which way. They can represent creatures, players, spells, items, artifacts, or basically anything you can think of.
