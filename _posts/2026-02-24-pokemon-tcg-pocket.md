## Cracking Packs

Pokemon TCG takes great lengths to deliver an exciting experience opening a pack.

When you open the app and you are able to open a pack (you can open one every 12 hours), you're immediately dropped onto the pack screen and notified that you can open a pack.

You press and confirm that you want to open a pack from the set you see in front of you.

You hit open and you're transported. Fifteen packs fly in and you can choose which one of these fifteen to open.

Now, I don't know if these packs are actually seeded with different cards, or if this is purely a song and dance, but it does succeed in allowing the player to feel like they're influencing a totally random decision that they have no real influence on.

You tap the specific pack you'd like, but now the magic happens. The app zoomes in on the top of the pack, and the cut line shimmers and entices you to rip it open.

You swipe your finger and the pack is cut according to your finger movement.

You finally open the pack! Five cards fly out, stacked on top of each other. You read a card, then move onto the next one.

The app tells you the rarity, and whether or not you're collecting a new card.

Once you review each card, you see all your new cards next to each other. The cards then fly into your collection.

You then register new cards into your `card dex` and they are dropped into the set of unique cards you have for that set.

The experience is seamless and captures every little joy you could get from cracking a pack of cards.



Pokemon TCG has a feature where you can look at opened packs from strangers and friends. You can then randomly select one of those cards to copy and keep (you don't steal it from them, of course). If you're pleased with your new card, you can send them a small token of your appreciation.

Of course, all the packs you open go to this system as well, and you can receive small tokens, too.

## Competition Is Not The Star

In many games, players consider tournaments or ladder climbing to be the greatest expression of skill and ability in a game, and thus the most sought after goal.

Pokemon TCG Pocket does have a ladder, and it does allow you to climb the ranks, but this does not take center stage.

You can look up the strongest net decks online.

One thing you'll notice is that many decks are considered competitive. Even if you want to win, you can still choose your Pokemon.
